Ad Mute for Streaming — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-23

Ad Mute for Streaming is a browser extension that mutes the current browser tab when it detects an ad break on a supported streaming site, and unmutes it when the show resumes.

Data collection

This extension does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data, browsing history, or video-watching activity. It has no analytics, no third-party trackers, and no remote servers of any kind. Everything it does happens locally in your browser.

What it stores, and where

The extension keeps a small amount of state in your browser's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local), which never leaves your device:

  • Which sites you've enabled/disabled the extension for
  • Whether debug logging is turned on
  • A short rolling log (last ~50 entries) of recent mute/unmute events, used only to show you activity in the extension's popup

None of this is synced to an account, sent to a server, or accessible to anyone but you.

Why it needs the permissions it asks for

  • tabs — to mute/unmute the specific browser tab playing the show, and to detect manual mute toggles.
  • storage — to save your on/off settings and the local activity log described above.
  • commands — to support the optional manual mute keyboard shortcut.
  • Host permissions on YouTube, Hulu, Disney+, Peacock, and Max/HBO Max — the extension needs to run a small script on these pages to look at the player's on-page state (CSS classes, visible text like "Ad 1 of 3", ad player elements) so it can tell an ad break from regular content. It does not read, store, or transmit anything else from these pages — no account info, no viewing history, no personal data.
